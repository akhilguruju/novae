School FAQs
Is it safe for school students?
Yes. The environment is secure, supervised by our staff, and structured with clearly defined zones for students and teachers. Safety is the first thing we plan around.
Are teachers required to accompany students?
Yes, at least one teacher per 15 students is recommended. Teachers are part of the experience and help manage their groups through each session.
Do you provide food?
Yes. Snacks, dinner, and breakfast are included. We can accommodate dietary requirements if informed in advance during booking.
What is the ideal group size?
We can accommodate groups of 20 to 80 students depending on the stay type selected. Contact us with your numbers and dates and we'll plan accordingly.

Astronomy Enthusiasts

One of the few dark-sky destinations near Hyderabad.

Novae Glamp is one of the few destinations near Hyderabad with minimal light pollution and open-sky access — making it a natural base for stargazers and astronomy lovers.

Our on-site telescope, guided sessions, and astronomy workshops create an experience that goes well beyond casual sky-watching. We align key events to meteor showers, lunar cycles, and planetary visibility windows.

A Typical Astronomy Night
7:00 PM Arrival & briefing — what to expect tonight, sky conditions
8:00 PM Dinner & equipment setup
9:00 PM Constellation identification session begins
10:00 PM Telescope time — individual viewing + guided observation
11:30 PM Astrophotography guidance & free observation
6:00 AM Optional: sunrise solar observation through filtered telescope
What Awaits You
  • Telescope sessions — deep sky objects, moon craters, planetary views
