Field notes

01

Give your eyes time

Full dark adaptation takes 20–30 minutes. Even a glance at a phone resets your night vision.

02

Check the Moon phase

A bright Moon washes out faint objects. New-Moon nights are best for the Milky Way.

03

Dress warm

Temperatures drop after dark, even in summer. You’ll be standing still for a while.

04

Look up, not through

The best view at Novae is naked-eye. The telescope adds detail; the wide sky is the show.
