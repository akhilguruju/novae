What you'll see

On a clear night at Novae, your naked eyes can resolve the Milky Way's dust lanes, the Andromeda galaxy as a faint smudge, and thousands of stars you've never seen from the city. With our telescopes, Saturn's rings, Jupiter's moons, and deep-sky nebulae come into focus.

Why darkness matters

Light pollution erases the night sky. The Bortle scale measures darkness from 1 (pristine) to 9 (city centre). Novae sits at Class 3 — rare this close to a major city.

1
Excellent Zodiacal light, gegenschein, zodiacal band all visible
2
Typical dark site Milky Way shows great detail and structure
3
Rural sky Milky Way clearly visible, some light domes on horizon
4
Rural/suburban Milky Way visible but loses detail
5–9
Suburban → City Milky Way faint to invisible, few stars

Guided sessions

Every night at Novae includes a guided stargazing session. Our hosts walk you through the visible constellations, point out planets and satellites, and set up telescopes for deep-sky viewing.

Naked-eye tour

Learn to navigate the sky by finding key constellations, the ecliptic, and the galactic plane. No equipment needed — just your eyes and 20 minutes of dark adaptation.

Telescope viewing

Observe planets, the Moon's craters, star clusters, and nebulae through our guided telescope setup. Targets vary by season and sky conditions.

Astrophotography intro

Bring your phone or camera — we'll help you capture the Milky Way, star trails, and long-exposure nightscapes. Tripods available on request.

Tips for your night

01

Give your eyes time

Full dark adaptation takes 20–30 minutes. Avoid phone screens — even a glance resets your night vision.

02

Check the Moon phase

A bright Moon washes out fainter objects. New Moon nights are best for the Milky Way and deep-sky viewing.

03

Dress warm

Temperatures drop after dark, even in summer. Bring a light jacket — you'll be standing still for a while.

04

Look up, not through

The best views at Novae are naked-eye. Binoculars and telescopes add detail, but the wide sky is the real show.
