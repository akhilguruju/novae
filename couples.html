Frequently Asked Questions

Couples FAQs

Which is the best stay for couples?
The Luxury Tepee Stay is our most romantic option — private pond, garden, AC, and complete privacy. The American Style Tents are boutique and intimate. The Boho Suite Rooms offer hotel-level comfort with the glamping experience. All three are excellent for couples.
Can you arrange a surprise proposal setup?
Yes — we handle these regularly. Share your vision with us and we'll plan the décor, the timing, the lighting and the private moment. All you have to do is show up. WhatsApp us with details at least 5–7 days before your visit.
Is it truly private — will there be other couples around?
We host multiple guests per night, but your stay area, your bonfire time and your stargazing session are all arranged to give you private space. The Tepee stay with private pond access is fully secluded from other guests.
What is included without add-ons?
Every couple stay includes accommodation, snacks on arrival, dinner, breakfast, guided stargazing with telescope access, and the bonfire evening. The add-ons (proposal décor, sound healing, private dinner setup, etc.) are optional enhancements we offer at extra charge.
How far in advance should we book?
For weekend stays, 1–2 weeks ahead is recommended. For Valentine's Day, New Year's Eve or special meteor shower nights, book 3–4 weeks ahead as these dates sell out early. Proposal and anniversary setups need at least 5 days lead time.
Can we have alcohol at the property?
Novae Glamp does not serve alcohol, but you are welcome to bring your own for personal consumption within your stay area. We'll ensure appropriate glassware and a private setup if you let us know in advance.

Ready to Plan Your Night Together?

Couple slots fill fast on weekends and new moon nights. Reach out and we'll plan everything.
