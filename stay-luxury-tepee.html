About This Stay

The Most Private Experience at Novae Glamp

The Luxury Tepee Stay is Novae Glamp's most complete experience — a beautifully styled tepee tent with full AC comfort, an attached washroom, and exclusive access to a private pond and garden that no other guests can enter. Designed entirely around privacy, intimacy and the night sky.

You arrive to a property that's already set — your pond, your garden, your private fire area. The evening unfolds at your own pace. Our guide comes to you for the stargazing session; everything else is yours to discover without a schedule.

What Makes It Different

🌊 Private Pond & Garden

A secluded pond with garden seating — accessible only to guests of this tepee. Watch the sky reflect on still water at night. Have breakfast beside it in the morning. No other guests, ever.

🏕 Tepee Architecture

The conical tepee structure is genuinely striking — tall canvas walls, warm internal lighting, a design that feels both wild and refined. Unlike any hotel room, unlike any regular glamping tent.

❄️ Full AC Comfort

A properly cooled interior on warm nights, with a real bed, quality linen and a level of comfort that makes waking up here genuinely enjoyable — not just a camping endurance test.

🔭 Sky Access from Your Own Lawn

Step directly outside your tepee onto your private lawn for stargazing. The tepee's position gives you open horizon access in all directions — you don't need to walk anywhere to see the Milky Way.

Tepee Stay FAQs

Is the pond completely private?
Yes — the pond and garden area is exclusively accessible to Tepee Stay guests. No other Novae Glamp guests can enter this zone. It's physically separated from the rest of the property.
Can we have a private bonfire by the pond?
Yes — a private fire setup at your pond area can be arranged on request at the time of booking. This is separate from the communal bonfire and includes dedicated wood supply and seating.
Can a third person stay in the tepee?
Yes — one extra adult or child can be accommodated. Please inform us at booking so we can arrange appropriate bedding. An additional charge applies per extra guest.
Is this suitable for a proposal?
It's our most popular stay for proposals — we've helped plan dozens here. Share your vision via WhatsApp at least 5–7 days ahead and we'll plan the setup, timing and any décor you'd like. You just show up.
