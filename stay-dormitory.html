About This Stay

The Most Budget-Friendly Way to Experience Novae Together

The Dormitory is Novae Glamp's group accommodation option — a spacious 320 sq.ft. air-conditioned room with a mix of single and bunk beds that sleeps 8 guests comfortably, up to 10 with extra bedding. It's the most cost-effective way for a large group to stay together at Novae without splitting into separate units.

Popular with school trips, corporate team outings and large friend groups, the dormitory delivers everything Novae Glamp is known for — guided stargazing, bonfire nights, activities — at the lowest per-person price on the property. The AC makes it genuinely comfortable year-round, while the group atmosphere makes every moment more social.

Capacity & Bed Configuration

🛏

Standard Configuration — 8 Guests

A mix of single beds and bunk beds arranged to give each guest their own sleeping space within the 320 sq.ft. room. Bedding, pillow and blanket provided for each guest. At ₹2,999/person, this works out to ₹23,992 for the whole group.

🪵

Extended Configuration — up to 10 Guests

Two extra mattresses on the floor can accommodate up to 10 guests total. This works well for groups who don't mind the closer quarters — and for school groups accustomed to shared sleeping arrangements.

What Makes It Work for Groups

❄️ Air-Conditioned — 320 sq.ft.

Unlike the hut tents, the dormitory is fully AC — making it comfortable in every season, including summer months when outdoor temperatures can be warm. A proper split AC unit cools the entire space effectively.

🚿 Shared Washroom Access

Clean shared washroom blocks with hot water, Western toilets and good lighting — serviced regularly throughout the day and night. Positioned close to the dormitory zone for convenience at night.

🔌 Group Charging Station

Multiple charging sockets available in the dormitory so every guest can charge devices overnight. Essential for school and corporate groups who need phones and cameras ready for the morning.

📦 Storage Space

Luggage storage space for bags, backpacks and school kitbags. Groups don't need to leave belongings outside or under beds — there's designated space to keep the room tidy and comfortable.

Dormitory FAQs

Can we book multiple dormitories for a larger group?
Yes — we have multiple dormitory units available. A group of 25 could book three dormitories, for example, with groups of 8–9 in each. We'll position them adjacent to each other and coordinate meals and activities as one group. Let us know your total headcount when enquiring.
Is the dormitory suitable for mixed-gender groups?
For school and corporate groups with mixed-gender requirements, we can allocate separate dormitory units for different groups — one for male guests, one for female guests. Please specify your requirements at the time of booking and we'll arrange accordingly.
Are there lockers or secure storage for valuables?
There are no individual lockers in the dormitory. The dormitory door has a lockable entry that the group controls. For school trips, we recommend teachers hold valuable items. The Novae Glamp property is fully enclosed and staffed — security has not been a concern for any group that has stayed.
Can teachers or group leaders stay in a different room type nearby?
Yes and this is very common for school trips. Teachers typically book hut tents or suite rooms next to the dormitory zone, giving them immediate proximity to students while having their own space. We coordinate the layout to keep teachers and students close at booking stage.
