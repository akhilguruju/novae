About This Stay

The Comfort You Need, the Sky You Came For

The Suite Rooms are for guests who want the full Novae Glamp experience without compromising on the kind of comfort a good hotel room provides. Three beautifully designed 240 sq.ft. rooms — each with boho interiors, king-size bed, AC, private washroom and a warm, layered aesthetic that feels genuinely considered.

Unlike tents, the suite rooms are permanent structures — solid walls, proper insulation, complete quiet. Walk outside your door and you're still under the same sky, at the same bonfire, with the same guide leading the same stargazing session as every other guest at Novae. The only difference is what you return to at the end of the evening.

Suite Room FAQs

How many suite rooms are there?
Three suite rooms are available — each with identical amenities and design. All three face the open property and sky. During peak nights, multiple rooms may be occupied simultaneously, though they are spaced for privacy.
Is it suitable for a family with a young child?
Yes — the sofa-cum-bed comfortably accommodates one extra child or adult. The solid walls, private washroom and AC make this the most family-friendly of the premium options. Inform us at booking so we can set up the extra sleeping arrangement before arrival.
How is it different from the American tent at the same price?
The American tent is a canvas structure — more dramatic visually, more of a tent feel, slightly closer to the outdoors. The suite room is a permanent structure — quieter, warmer in winter, cooler in summer, with more interior space. Both offer the same amenity level and both have private washrooms. Your preference depends on whether you want a tent experience or a room experience.
Is there access to the stargazing area?
Yes — all suite room guests have full access to the communal stargazing observation area, bonfire, activities and all facilities. The suite room guests join the guided stargazing session at the same time as other guests, or can arrange a private session at an additional charge.
