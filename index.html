Novae Glamp began with a simple question: what if the best night of your life was only 30 minutes away? Nestled in the quiet countryside of Bhuvanagiri, we created a place where the Milky Way isn't a photograph — it's your ceiling.

Every detail here is designed to slow you down. To make you look up. To remind you what silence sounds like.
