Our Story

A Stargazing Retreat Designed for Meaningful Escapes

Somewhere between busy schedules and city lights, we stopped noticing the sky. Most people today have never seen a sky full of stars — not because they don't want to, but because they don't get the chance.

Novae Glamp started with a simple idea: what if we could bring people closer to something they've been missing all along? Today, it's a space where families, couples, and groups step away from the noise, experience stargazing for the first time, and reconnect with what actually matters.
