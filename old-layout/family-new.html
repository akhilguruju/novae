Family & Friends
Create Nights You’ll Talk About for Years

Step away from screens, noise, and routine— and gather under a sky full of stars. Stay in beautifully lit tents, laugh around a glowing bonfire, and experience the magic of stargazing together. From spotting constellations to late-night conversations, this is where friends and families connect in the most unforgettable way.

When was the last time everyone was truly present?

  • No phones. No distractions.

  • Just laughter, conversations, and moments that actually stay.

At Novae Glamp, families bond deeper and friends reconnect— under a sky full of stars.

Why Families & Friends Love This
  • Open space for kids to play freely
  • Safe, secure, and monitored environment
  • Activities for all age groups
  • No pressure itinerary—relax at your pace
  • A rare chance to disconnect and bond
