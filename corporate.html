🥁 Drum Circle

No musical experience needed — everyone plays, everyone contributes. Within 20 minutes a group of colleagues finds a shared beat. Drum circles break hierarchy naturally and completely. The most consistently powerful team activity we run, regardless of company size or culture.

🎶 Sound Healing Session

Guided Tibetan bowl meditation for the entire team — outdoors, under open sky. Designed to genuinely decompress from work stress. Teams that arrive exhausted often report this as the moment they actually started to feel present.

🔭 Stargazing Together

A shared moment of awe — the same sky, the same wonder, regardless of who reports to whom. Looking at Saturn's rings together does something to a team's dynamic that no facilitated session can replicate.

🔥 Bonfire & Community Jam

Around a real fire with live music — the most natural conversation space there is. No agenda, no slides, no structured facilitation. Just people talking like people. This is where relationships actually form.

Frequently Asked Questions

Corporate Outing FAQs

What is the minimum and maximum team size?
We accommodate teams from 15 to 80 people in a single overnight outing. For teams above 80, we can discuss a private two-night exclusive booking of the entire property. Smaller groups of 8–14 can also be accommodated with customised programming.
Is there a meeting/boardroom space available?
Novae Glamp does not have a conventional boardroom setup. If your outing requires a structured facilitated session (strategy, planning, team dialogue), we can arrange seating in our open-air space with basic AV support. We're best suited for teams that want to step away from work — not bring it outdoors.
Can alcohol be served at a corporate event?
Novae Glamp does not serve alcohol. Teams are welcome to bring their own for personal consumption. For formal corporate events where alcohol service is a requirement, please flag this at enquiry stage and we'll discuss what's feasible.
How do we get a formal proposal for our accounts team?
WhatsApp or email with your team size, preferred dates and any specific requirements. We'll send a full proposal document with programme outline, per-head pricing, inclusions and GST invoice details within 24 hours — suitable for internal approval and company accounts.
What is the cancellation and rescheduling policy?
Cancellation 14+ days before: full refund. 7–14 days: 50% refund or free reschedule. Less than 7 days: reschedule credit valid 90 days. We understand corporate dates shift — we're flexible on rescheduling as long as we have reasonable notice.
Can we do a day outing without staying overnight?
Yes — day outing packages (10 AM–6 PM) are available for corporate groups. These include team activities, lunch, creative workshops and an introduction to astronomy. Stargazing is only available on overnight trips. Day outings are popular for larger teams who want the experience without the overnight logistics.
