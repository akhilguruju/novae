About This Stay

The Right Balance of Comfort and Camping

The Hut Style Tents are the sweet spot at Novae Glamp — solid enough to feel like a real shelter, open enough to feel like you're actually camping. A hut structure with proper rain protection, comfort bedding, a fan and charging sockets. Not a luxury room, not a thin canvas tent. Something deliberately in between.

Available as 2-sharing, 3-sharing and 5-sharing configurations, the hut tents are the most versatile option at Novae — perfect for families who want everyone together, friend groups who don't need separate rooms, and groups who want the full outdoor experience without giving up basic comforts.

What Makes It Work

🏗 Solid Hut Structure

Not a canvas tent — a proper hut structure with solid walls and a tensile cloth roof that handles rain without drama. You sleep through the night, not around it.

🛏 Comfort Bedding

Proper mattresses with clean bedding — not sleeping bags on a mat. You wake up actually rested, which matters when you've been up until midnight watching stars.

🔌 Fan & Charging

A standing fan for warm nights and a charging socket so your phone (and camera) are ready for the sunrise. Small details that make the difference for group stays.

🚿 Clean Shared Washrooms

Maintained washroom blocks close to the hut zone — hot water, clean toilets, proper lighting. Shared, but never uncomfortable. Cleaned multiple times daily.

Hut Tent FAQs

How many hut tents can a group book together?
You can book as many huts as your group needs — and we'll assign them adjacent to each other so your group stays together on the property. A group of 15 could book three 5-sharing huts side by side, for example. Mention your total group size when enquiring and we'll configure the best layout.
Is there AC in the hut tents?
The hut tents have standing fans, not AC. Novae Glamp is at an elevation that makes most nights pleasant without AC. During peak summer months (April–June), we recommend the suite rooms or American tents if AC is a priority. From October through March, the hut tents are very comfortable with the fan.
Are the shared washrooms close to the huts?
Yes — the washroom blocks are positioned within comfortable walking distance of the hut zone. They are cleaned multiple times daily, have hot water at all hours, and are well-lit at night. Guests consistently mention the cleanliness of washrooms in their reviews.
Can we mix hut sizes — one 2-sharing and one 3-sharing for the same group?
Absolutely. Many groups mix configurations — a couple in a 2-sharing hut and three friends in a 3-sharing next door, for example. Just mention your group breakdown when enquiring and we'll arrange the huts accordingly.
