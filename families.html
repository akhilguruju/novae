Frequently Asked Questions

Families & Friends FAQs

Is it safe for young children?
Yes. The property is enclosed, well-lit in common areas, and our team is present throughout the night. We've hosted families with children as young as 2 years old without any issues.
What is the minimum and maximum group size?
We can host groups from 4 people to 100 people in a single night across all stay types. For groups above 30, we recommend booking at least 2 weeks in advance.
Are meals included for everyone?
Yes — snacks on arrival, a full dinner, and a hearty breakfast the next morning are included for every guest regardless of which stay type they've booked. Dietary requirements can be accommodated with advance notice.
What if it's cloudy and we can't see stars?
The bonfire, games, music, and all other activities continue regardless of weather. If stargazing is not possible, we offer an indoor astronomy session with projector and star maps. You can also reschedule your stargazing night at no extra charge.
Can we bring our own food or alcohol?
Outside food is not permitted as all meals are included. Alcohol is not served on the property, but you may bring your own for personal consumption within your stay area.
How far is it from Hyderabad and how do we get there?
Novae Glamp is 30 km from Hyderabad city centre — approximately 45–55 minutes by car via NH65. We'll share the Google Maps pin at the time of booking confirmation. No public transport available; self-drive or cab recommended.
