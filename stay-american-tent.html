About This Stay

Boutique Canvas Glamping, Done Properly

Inspired by traditional native American tent architecture, these canvas tents have a structural character unlike any other accommodation at Novae Glamp — or anywhere near Hyderabad. The tall conical form, paired with full AC, attached washroom and premium bedding, creates a genuinely boutique experience that feels both wild and refined.

Limited units mean you're unlikely to have neighbours immediately beside you. It's the right amount of privacy for a couple who want a beautiful space without the full exclusivity price of the tepee. Step inside and you find a hotel room. Step outside and you're under a proper dark sky.

What Makes It Different

🏕 Distinctive Canvas Architecture

The American-style canvas structure is architecturally striking — tall, purposeful and unlike any hotel room or standard tent. The form itself becomes part of the experience. Guests consistently comment on it before anything else.

❄️ Full AC with Hotel-Level Comfort

Split AC unit, king-size bed with quality linen, attached washroom with hot water — the canvas exterior is the glamping side. Everything inside is the comfort side. You don't choose between the two here.

🔭 2 Minutes from the Observation Area

The American tents are positioned for easy access to the main stargazing observation zone — close enough to walk out at 11 PM for a personal session, far enough from communal areas to feel private.

🌅 Best Photography Backdrop

The tent's structural silhouette against a sunrise or sunset sky is one of the most photographed scenes at Novae Glamp. Guests with cameras consistently get their best travel shots from outside these tents.

American Tent FAQs

How many units are available?
We have a limited number of American Style Tent units — this is intentional. Fewer units means more space between guests, a quieter experience and the boutique feel this stay is known for. Book early, especially for weekends.
Is it suitable for 3 people?
Yes — one extra adult or child can be accommodated on a mattress or sofa bed. Please inform us at booking. An additional per-guest charge applies for the extra person covering their meals and activities.
How is it different from the Tepee Stay?
The Tepee Stay includes a fully private pond and garden — a secluded zone accessible only to that unit. The American Tents are equally comfortable but share the main property space. The tepee is for guests who want complete seclusion; the American tent is for guests who want boutique comfort and great style.
Is there a private outdoor area?
Each American tent has a dedicated outdoor seating area at its entrance — not enclosed, but personally assigned to your tent. You're unlikely to have other guests immediately beside you given the limited unit count.
