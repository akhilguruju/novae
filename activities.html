⭐ What We're Built Around

Guided Stargazing Sessions

Every Novae stay includes a guided stargazing session led by our astronomy host. Identify constellations, track visible planets, and observe through our telescope. On a clear night, guests can see over 6,500 stars with the naked eye alone.

  • Constellation identification — Orion, Scorpius, Cassiopeia & more
  • Telescope viewing — Saturn's rings, Jupiter's moons, Orion Nebula
  • Planet tracking — Jupiter, Saturn, Mars, Venus visible to naked eye
  • Milky Way viewing on new moon nights
  • Astrophotography guidance for camera users
  • Special sessions on meteor shower nights

All Activities

The Full Novae Experience

All activities below are included with your overnight stay. Some can be added to day-visit packages — ask us when booking.

🔥
Evening · All Groups
Bonfire Evenings

Gather around a real campfire with music, storytelling, and woodsmoke. The social heart of every Novae stay — conversations here go differently.

🌿
Morning · All Groups
Nature Walks

Sunrise and sunset trails with a local naturalist — flora, fauna, birds, and quiet that city life erases. Available as guided or self-paced walks.

🎯
Evening · Groups & Families
Outdoor Games

Box cricket, human foosball, tug-of-war, relay races — high-energy team games that work for all ages and group sizes without any special equipment.

🎨
Afternoon · All Groups
Art & Pottery Workshops

Hands-on creative sessions — clay pottery, canvas painting, or guided art projects under open skies. Great for all ages, no experience needed.

🥁
Evening · Corporates & Schools
Drum Circle

High-energy group activity that builds rhythm, unity, and full participation. Everyone plays — no prior experience needed. Popular with corporate teams and school groups for breaking barriers fast.

🧘
Morning / Evening · All Groups
Sound Healing Sessions

Guided Tibetan sound bowl meditation for relaxation and mindfulness — for couples, wellness groups, or corporate teams who need to decompress before they can truly bond.

🚀
Afternoon · Schools & Students
Water Rockets

STEM activity where students build and launch water-powered rockets — applying physics, working in teams, and having actual fun in the process. Designed specifically for school groups.

🎵
Evening · All Groups
Live Music Sessions

Curated live music evenings around the bonfire — acoustic performances that set the tone for a night of open conversations and shared memories. Available on select nights.

🦅
Morning · Families & Schools
Bird Watching

Early morning guided bird watching with binoculars through the property's natural landscape. Over 40 species have been spotted at various times of year.

🌅
Morning · All Guests
Sunrise Observation

Wake up early and watch the landscape transform from night to morning. On clear days, a filtered solar telescope is set up for safe sun observation — sunspots visible on good days.

🏏
Evening · Groups
Box Cricket

Cricket played in a compact boxed pitch — fast-paced, inclusive, and endlessly entertaining. Works for mixed-age groups and creates friendly competition that carries into the bonfire conversations.

🎲
Anytime · All Groups
Indoor Games & Carrom

A relaxed indoor game zone for downtime — carrom, board games, and card games available throughout the stay for guests who want a quieter alternative between activities.
