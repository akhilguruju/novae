About Novae Glamp

Where the City Ends,
the Sky Begins

Novae Glamp isn't just a place you visit — it's a place you feel. Built around the idea that we've all forgotten to look up, this is where nights slow down, conversations deepen, and the sky becomes part of your experience again.

Our Story

It Started with a Simple Realisation

Somewhere between busy schedules and city lights, we stopped noticing the sky. Most people today have never seen a sky full of stars — not because they don't want to, but because they don't get the chance.

"What if we could bring people closer to something they've been missing all along?"

What began as a small setup turned into something bigger — a space where people could step away from noise, experience stargazing for the first time, and reconnect with what actually matters.

Today, Novae Glamp is not just about stays. It's about creating moments of awe, peace, and connection — for families, couples, school groups, and corporate teams. 30 km from Hyderabad. A world away from everything else.
